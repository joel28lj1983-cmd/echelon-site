What to include

A few details help us give you a useful reply on the first pass.

  • Roughly how many people are in your organization.
  • Whether your contracts mention DFARS 252.204-7012, FAR 52.204-21, or a CMMC level.
  • Whether you handle Federal Contract Information or Controlled Unclassified Information.
  • Whether a prime or the DoD has asked about your CMMC status or SPRS score.
  • Any deadline you are working against.
Please do not send sensitive, classified, or controlled information by email. A short description of your situation is all we need to suggest a starting point.

  • About the firm

Echelon Advisory Group LLC provides CMMC readiness advisory services, including gap assessments, SSP and POA&M development, and assessment preparation. Echelon is led by a CMMC Registered Practitioner (CPN 73205). Echelon is not a C3PAO, does not perform certification assessments, and does not issue CMMC certifications. Certification decisions rest with accredited assessors and the DoD. Copyright 2026 Echelon Advisory Group LLC.
