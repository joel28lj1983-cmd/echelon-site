Region

The Gulf Coast Defense Corridor

From Pensacola in the west to Panama City in the east, Northwest Florida holds one of the densest concentrations of defense installations in the Southeast. This is where Echelon works.

Home ground

A corridor of Navy and Air Force installations anchors a large ecosystem of contractors and subcontractors along this stretch of coast. Many of them are small firms that now carry CMMC and NIST SP 800-171 obligations flowing down from their primes.

Being based in the corridor means we can meet clients on-site and we understand the contracts and prime relationships that shape the local market. The installations below are described only to explain that market. Echelon does not work on behalf of any of them.

Installations and their contractor ecosystems

Each installation supports a distinct base of local contractors. The notes below describe the kind of work concentrated around each one.

Eglin AFB

One of the Air Force's largest armament, research, and flight-test enterprises, Eglin draws engineering, test and evaluation, and munitions sustainment contractors, many of them small firms handling controlled technical data.

Hurlburt Field

As the headquarters of Air Force Special Operations Command, Hurlburt supports a contractor base in aircraft modification, intelligence and reconnaissance support, and training and simulation.

NAS Pensacola

A center of naval aviation training, and of information warfare training at nearby Corry Station, NAS Pensacola surrounds itself with aviation maintenance, training, and information technology contractors.

NAS Whiting Field

One of the Navy's primary flight-training installations, Whiting Field sustains a local base of aircraft maintenance, flight-training support, and facilities contractors.

Tyndall AFB

Rebuilding as a modern installation after Hurricane Michael, Tyndall has drawn a heavy concentration of construction, facilities, and base-support contractors working under federal contract requirements.

NSA Panama City / NSWC PCD

Naval Support Activity Panama City is home to the Naval Surface Warfare Center Panama City Division, whose work in littoral and expeditionary systems supports engineering and RDT&E contractors handling controlled unclassified information.
