CMMC Level 1 Self-Check

CMMC Level 1 covers 17 basic safeguarding practices that apply to companies handling Federal Contract Information. Answer the 17 plain questions below to see where you stand and which items need work.

This check runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you answer is saved, stored, or sent anywhere.

For each question, choose the answer that best fits your organization today. If you are not sure, "Partial" is the honest choice. There are no wrong answers here; the point is to see the picture clearly.

Get the documented version

This quick check is a starting point, not an assessment. A readiness snapshot from Echelon walks each practice with your real evidence and hands you a written summary you can act on and use for your SPRS self-assessment.

Email for a readiness snapshot

About this result. This self-check is an informal educational estimate based only on the answers you selected. It is not a CMMC assessment, not an official score, and not a substitute for your SPRS self-assessment. For Level 1, all 17 practices must be met to attest compliance; there is no partial credit and no plan-of-action allowance at this level.
