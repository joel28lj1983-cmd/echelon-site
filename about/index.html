Joel Clark owns Echelon Advisory Group LLC in Gulf Breeze, Florida. The firm is owner-operated. He handles each review. Clients are served remotely.

The work is an independent review of completed and closing construction projects, primarily for specialty trade contractors and subcontractors. Echelon reconciles the project, commercial, and financial records: the change-order log, extra-work direction, T&M tickets, pay applications, invoices, job-cost, retainage, backcharges, and closeout. The review shows what is closed, unresolved, missing, or awaiting a decision.

You provide the project context and help locate records. Echelon compares them and writes what they support, and what they do not. The review does not start from a target recovery number.

Boundaries

Not your lawyer, CPA, engineer, or claims consultant.

No legal advice or representation. No engineering opinion. No accounting opinion. No schedule-delay analysis. No expert testimony. No guarantee that money will be collected. Echelon does not replace your project team. If a question belongs with one of those professionals, the review names the issue and stops.
