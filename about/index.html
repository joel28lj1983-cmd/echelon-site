About Echelon

A focused advisory firm built around disciplined project review.

Echelon Advisory Group LLC is based in Gulf Breeze, Florida and serves clients remotely.

The Firm

A defined focus. Direct accountability.

Echelon is a founder-led advisory firm focused on completed and closing construction projects. The work is deliberately specific: reconstruct the commercial record, reconcile it to the final account, identify unresolved items, and give management a clearer basis for deciding what deserves action.

Echelon is owner-operated, and each engagement is reviewed directly by Joel Clark. The work is built around disciplined record review, commercial reconciliation, clear documentation, and direct management-level findings. The objective is simple: establish what the project record supports, identify what remains unresolved, and give management a clear basis for the next decision.

Operating Standard

Detailed internally. Simple for the client.

Echelon organizes and compares the records, documents unresolved items, and brings specific questions to management. Your team provides project context and helps locate missing records.

Boundaries

Know exactly where the work stops.

Echelon does not provide legal advice, legal representation, engineering opinions, accounting opinions, schedule-delay analysis, expert testimony, or guaranteed collection outcomes. When a review identifies a matter requiring one of those judgments, Echelon isolates the issue and leaves the determination with the appropriate professional.
