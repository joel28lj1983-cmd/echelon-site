The Echelon Review

One completed project. A documented view of what remains unresolved.

Your project team has moved to current work. Accounting has a final balance. The change log has its own history. Email, field tickets, proposals, pay applications, invoices, and closeout records may tell different parts of the same story.

Echelon brings those records together. The review identifies material items that reconcile, items that remain commercially open, documentation gaps, and matters that should be handed to another professional.

The objective is simple: management should know whether the project is actually closed.

How a completed-project review works

Where Echelon Fits

Between project completion and commercial closure.

Existing records

The review works from the records your company already maintains. Your team provides context and access through an agreed transfer method.

Independent review

Material items are traced across project, commercial, and financial records. Conclusions reflect the available evidence and its limits.

Informed next steps

Management receives a clear account of open items and documentation gaps, with matters identified for counsel, accounting, engineering, or claims specialists.

Where We Look

The answer is rarely in one system.

Material project history can be split across field records, change logs, email, pricing files, pay applications, invoices, job-cost records, and closeout documents. A review is useful only if those sources are compared rather than read in isolation.

01

03

What reached contract value, billing, payment, retainage, deductions, closeout, and the final balance.

Common Questions

Do we need to know exactly what is missing before contacting Echelon?

No. A reasonable starting point is a completed or closing project where management is not confident that the final commercial account tells the full story.

Does Echelon replace our attorney, CPA, engineer, claims consultant, or project team?

No. Echelon handles record reconstruction and commercial review. Legal, accounting, engineering, schedule, damages, and other professional judgments remain with the appropriate professional.

Do we need to buy or implement software?

No. The initial review works from the records your company already has.

Can we start with one project?

Yes. That is the preferred first engagement.
