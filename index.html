The records

The job is not in one file.

The crew has moved on. Accounting has a balance. The change-order log has its own history. Email, extra-work direction, T&M tickets, proposals, pay applications, invoices, job-cost, and closeout records can disagree. Echelon reads them against each other.

The review uses the records you already keep. You provide the context and the files, through a transfer method agreed before anything is sent. A finding goes only as far as those records support.

Questions

Do we need to know exactly what is missing before contacting Echelon?

No. Start with a completed or closing project when you are not confident the final account tells the whole story.

Does Echelon replace our attorney, CPA, engineer, claims consultant, or project team?

No. Echelon reconstructs and reconciles the records. Legal, accounting, engineering, schedule, damages, and claims judgments stay with those professionals.

Do we need to buy or implement software?

No. The review uses the records you already have.

Can we start with one project?

Yes. That is the first engagement.
